% Figure (Section 4.1, a): the architecture of Reverb in an AI-RAN server and the procedure for a failed TB.  Gray: what
% exists without Reverb (L1, L2 scheduler, the GPUs of the server, AI inference); orange: what Reverb adds (its three
% components and the paths they open).  Running example: failed TBs A and B of cell 1 and C of cell 2.
% (1) the received samples of a slot with a failed TB stay in GPU memory; (2) Recovery Gate: A and C failed at most K
% code blocks and wait for their recovery, B failed all ten and gets its grant at once; (3) Receiver Selector: cell 2 has
% interference above theta and uses the NRx trained with interference, cell 1 the NRx trained without it (published);
% (4) Recovery Scheduler: one recovery per GPU, rejection after D - r; (5) each GPU decodes its TB again from the stored
% samples between the L1 slots; (6) the result goes to the L2 scheduler, which holds the grants of A and C, schedules the
% retransmission of B in the next uplink slot, and delivers A and C once decoded.  No measured value appears.
