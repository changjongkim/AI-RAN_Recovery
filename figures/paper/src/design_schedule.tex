% Figure (Section 4.3): admission by the latest start time on one GPU.  Failed TBs 1 and 2 come from one uplink slot,
% TB 3 from the next slot and TB 4 from the slot after it; each has the deadline D of its slot (11.0 ms after the slot)
% and needs the run time r (4.3 ms), so it must start by D - r.  In arrival order (as in Section 3.4), TB 3 starts after
% its latest start, runs past its deadline and holds the GPU, so TB 4 also ends past its deadline.  Reverb rejects TB 3
% when its latest start passes (the base station sends its grant at once) and recovers TB 4 by its deadline.  With the
% same deadline rule in every cell, the deadline order equals the arrival order; the difference is the rejection.
% Geometry follows the timing of Section 5.1 (slots every 2.5 ms); no value is printed.
