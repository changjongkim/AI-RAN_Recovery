% Figure (Section 4.3): the recoveries of failed TBs of cells A, B and C on the two GPUs of a server, in the order of
% arrival (as in Section 3.4) and with Recovery Scheduler.  The index of a TB is its uplink slot; each TB has the deadline
% D of its slot and needs the run time r, so it must start by D - r.  In arrival order, B1 starts after its latest start,
% misses its deadline and holds GPU 1 while C3 waits, so C3 misses its deadline as well.  Recovery Scheduler runs C0
% before A1 and B1 (earliest deadline first), rejects B1 when its latest start passes (its grant goes out at once), and
% recovers every other TB in time.  Geometry follows the timing of Section 5.1 (slots every 2.5 ms, deadline 11.0 ms
% after the slot, a run of 4.3 ms); no value is printed.
