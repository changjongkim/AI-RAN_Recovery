% Figure (Section 4.3): deadline scheduling of recoveries over the GPUs of a server.  Failed TBs of cells A, B and C
% arrive at the L1 results of their slots; each has the deadline D of its slot and needs the run time r of its receiver,
% so it must start by its latest start time D - r.  Each GPU runs one recovery at a time; when a GPU becomes free it takes
% the waiting TB with the earliest deadline.  B1 waits behind C0 and A1, its latest start passes before a GPU becomes free,
% and the scheduler rejects it: the base station requests its retransmission at once.  The geometry follows the timing
% of Section 5.1 (slots every 2.5 ms, deadline 11.0 ms after the slot, a run of 4.3 ms) but no value is printed.
