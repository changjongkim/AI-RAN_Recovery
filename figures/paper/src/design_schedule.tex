% Figure (Section 4.3): admission by the latest start time on one GPU.  Failed TBs 1 and 2 come from one uplink slot,
% TB 3 from the next slot and TB 4 from the slot after it.  Each TB can start its recovery from its L1 result until its
% latest start time D - r and must finish by its deadline D (11.0 ms after its slot; the run time is drawn as the 4.3 ms
% of the network alone to keep the example short).  In arrival order without rejection, TB 3 starts after its latest start, runs past its
% deadline and holds the GPU, so TB 4 also ends past its deadline: 2 of 4 TBs are recovered in time.  Reverb rejects
% TB 3 at its latest start (its grant goes out at once) and recovers TB 4: 3 of 4 in time.  With the same deadline rule
% in every cell, the deadline order equals the arrival order; the difference is the rejection.  No value is printed.
