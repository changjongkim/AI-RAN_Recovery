% Figure (Section 4.3): deadline scheduling of recoveries over the GPUs of a server.  Failed TBs of cells A, B and C
% arrive at the L1 results of their slots (the index is the slot); each has the deadline D of its slot and needs the run
% time r of its receiver, so it must start by its latest start time D - r.  Each GPU runs one recovery at a time; when a
% GPU becomes free it takes the waiting TB with the earliest deadline (C0 before A1 and B1).  B1 waits behind C0 and A1,
% its latest start passes before a GPU becomes free, and the scheduler rejects it: the base station sends its grant at
% once.  The geometry follows the timing of Section 5.1 (slots every 2.5 ms, deadline 11.0 ms after the slot, a run of
% 4.3 ms) but no value is printed.

    \node[rowh, minimum width=1.9cm, minimum height=0.34cm, font=\tabsize\bfseries] at (0.95, \y) {\t};
  }
  \begin{scope}[xshift=2.15cm, x=0.7cm]
    \def\r{4.3}
