% Figure (Section 4.1, b): the recovery deadline bounds the cost of waiting; D_0 = U_s - L marks the last grant without delay.  A failed TB arrives in an uplink slot and
% its L1 result (CRC fail) is known at t_L1.  Conventional: the grant follows at t_L1 and the retransmission uses the
% first uplink slot U_s at least the grant lead L later.  With Reverb the TB waits for its recovery.  If the recovery
% fails, the grant follows the recovery result.  In the latest case the GPU still runs the recovery of an earlier failed
% TB (of this or another cell) at t_L1, and the recovery of this TB starts only at its latest start D - r and ends at D;
% if it fails, the grant at D still reaches U_r = D + L, the latest uplink slot the operator allows.  So waiting delays
% the retransmission of a TB that recovery does not decode by at most U_r - U_s.  Geometry follows the timing of
% Section 5.1 (L1 result 1.4 ms after the slot, recovery 6.3 ms, DDDSU, grant lead 4 ms, D = 11.0 ms); no value printed.
