% Figure (Section 4.1, b): the recovery deadline bounds the cost of waiting.  A failed TB arrives in an uplink slot and
% its L1 result is known at t_L1.  Conventional: the grant follows at t_L1 and the retransmission uses the first uplink
% slot U_s at least the grant lead L later.  With Reverb the TB waits for its recovery: if the recovery fails, the grant
% follows the recovery result; a recovery that starts at its latest start D - r ends at D, and the grant at D still
% reaches U_r = D + L, the latest uplink slot the operator allows for the retransmission.  So waiting delays the
% retransmission of a TB that recovery does not decode by at most U_r - U_s.  Geometry follows the timing of Section 5.1
% (L1 result 1.4 ms after the slot, recovery 6.3 ms, DDDSU, grant lead 4 ms, D = 11.0 ms); no value is printed.
