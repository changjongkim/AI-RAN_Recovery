% Figure (Section 4.2): the same three failed TBs without Reverb (gray) and with Reverb (orange).  A and B are TBs of
% cell 1, C of cell 2, which has interference from neighbor cells above theta.  Conventional: every failed TB gets its
% grant at the L1 result, is sent again in the next uplink slot U_s after the grant lead, and the L1 decodes the
% retransmission; the TB is delivered at the L1 result of U_s.  Reverb: Recovery Gate lets A (2 of 10 code blocks
% failed) and C (3 failed) wait for their recovery, which ends before the retransmission would be decoded: they are
% delivered earlier and the uplink slot carries no retransmission.  B failed all ten code blocks and gets its grant at
% once, as without Reverb, while its recovery runs in parallel.  Receiver Selector runs the NRx trained with interference
% for C and the NRx trained without it for A.  Geometry follows the timing of Section 5.1 (L1 result 1.4 ms after the
% slot, recovery 6.3 ms, DDDSU, grant lead 4 ms); no value is printed.
